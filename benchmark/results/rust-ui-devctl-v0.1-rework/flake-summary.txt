Flake characterization (H3b) - devctl_windows CAPTURE_FAILED guard

Observed: one CAPTURE_FAILED ("retained semantic geometry/state changed
during WGC capture") at "steady capture 1" in 1 of 17 runs total.

Runs (cargo test --locked --features devtools --test devctl_windows):
  current tree (cf8ab91 content):  8/8 pass   (h3b-flake/run-1..8.txt)
  baseline 7a1ad19 (scratch worktree): 8/8 pass (h3b-flake/base-1..8.txt)
  raw logs kept at %TEMP%\h3b-flake\ during investigation.

Cause: NOT proven - no failure reproduced while instrumented.
Mechanism analysis (code reachability, verified against 7a1ad19):
  - The failing variant is the snapshot-mismatch CAPTURE_FAILED - the two
    snapshot-layout calls inside `screenshot` must bracket a change in a
    structural field (hovered/focused/pressed/rect/...).
  - Tooltip scheduler: eliminated (probe has no tooltips; tooltips are
    separate HWNDs, not snapshot nodes).
  - Caret blink: eliminated for this step (focus was on probe.button;
    caret state is not in the snapshot).
  - OS WM_KILLFOCUS: eliminated (send_focused forwards to the focused
    PEER only; be.focus never clears -> `focused` cannot flip).
  - Verified reachable: a real OS WM_MOUSEMOVE/WM_MOUSELEAVE delivered to
    the shown probe window flips `be.hot` -> `hovered` in the snapshot
    (devtools.rs dev_node reads `self.hot`). WM_LBUTTONDOWN could flip
    pressed/focused likewise. The probe is a real 980x900 window on a
    live desktop; any parked-cursor jitter can deliver one mid-capture.
    `hovered` field + the two-snapshot guard are byte-identical at
    7a1ad19 -> the susceptibility predates this rework.
  - The H1/H2 edits only add drains/wait-idles that shrink the unstable
    window and `leave` reduces persistent hover state.

Conclusion: pre-existing environmental flake (real OS input reaching the
shown probe window mid-WGC-capture); 8/8 vs 8/8 runs cannot prove equal
rates at ~6% event frequency, but no evidence of a regression.

Recommended (described, not applied - out of scope): bounded retry inside
the CLI screenshot arm (wait-idle, re-snapshot, re-capture x2) before
failing CAPTURE_FAILED - transient input produces a one-shot mismatch
while a genuinely mutating scene keeps mismatching.
